THE OTHER

Desperate to pay off a loan shark, two burglars commit a routine home invasion, only to be forced into a self-defense shooting that unleashes a hidden horror into the city.

DRAFT 1

43 Pages. Horror. (05/20/2026)

This script was originally intended to be a Venom fan-film script. The original short I wrote had Venom in it. The title "THE OTHER" comes from that. This is the first draft. So, it's not perfect at all. But, it's something I hold dearly to my heart. It's not as personal as maybe other works, but I have had a strange attatchment to this script.

I also tried to make this 90 pages. So, if you're wondering why it's only 43 -- I didn't really write enough plot nor did I put enough text. I wrote this with 0 filler in mind so I made sure that there was 0 filler words. But, in doing that, I winded up just making the scenes bare and unimaginative. Which was stupid of me.

The inspiration after I wrote the Venom short script was Zach Cregger's Resident Evil. I actually felt like I needed a new script to write, and at that point I had gotten rid of the Venom refrences because I wanted to ask for feedback on Reddit, but after that... I kinda didn't know what to do. I constantly need to write something so I was just looking for something to write. I had shitty outlines of fan stories. But, man... I needed something original. Don't ask how, but I somehow came in contact with the script. I read it in like a day or two, immediately after reading I had a realization. I knew what my script was going to take inspiration from.

DRAFT 2

57 Pages. Horror. (06/08/2026)

This draft has some issues. There were some things I overlooked while writing this draft. I was sleep deprived majority of the time. It's stronger, but not by much. This was when I first put in the new character DETECTIVE BURCH. He's in the 3rd draft and I do like his addition in the 3rd draft. In this, he was just a shitty copy of Detective Loki from "Prisoners."

As I write this description, on 11/11/2026, I have put the script aside for a little while to start writing more scripts (both short and features) and to get a fresh pair of eyes when I return. I love this story but it needs some time away from me.
